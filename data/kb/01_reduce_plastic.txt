TOPIC: Reducing Plastic Use at Home and Daily Life
SOURCE: Environmental Best Practices Guide

== SINGLE-USE PLASTICS ==
Single-use plastics are one of the biggest sources of household waste. Common culprits include plastic bags, straws, water bottles, coffee cups, cutlery, food packaging, and cling wrap. Each year over 380 million metric tonnes of plastic is produced globally; only 9% is recycled.

ACTIONABLE TIPS – KITCHEN:
1. Switch to beeswax wraps or silicone lids instead of cling wrap/plastic wrap.
2. Use glass, stainless steel, or ceramic food containers for storage.
3. Buy loose fruit and vegetables instead of pre-packaged versions.
4. Use a reusable shopping bag (cotton, jute, or canvas) every time you shop.
5. Choose products in cardboard, glass, or metal packaging over plastic.
6. Make your own cleaning products (vinegar + baking soda) to avoid plastic spray bottles.
7. Use bar soap and shampoo bars instead of liquid products in plastic bottles.
8. Invest in a water filter jug and a reusable stainless steel bottle to eliminate single-use water bottles.

ACTIONABLE TIPS – BATHROOM:
1. Switch to bamboo toothbrushes (biodegradable handle).
2. Use toothpaste tablets or toothpaste in glass jars.
3. Replace plastic razors with safety razors (last for years; only blade is replaced).
4. Choose refillable deodorant sticks or natural crystal deodorant.
5. Use solid conditioner bars alongside shampoo bars.
6. Select cotton buds with paper stems instead of plastic.

ACTIONABLE TIPS – ON THE GO:
1. Carry a reusable coffee cup (saves 500+ disposable cups per year per person).
2. Bring reusable cutlery in your bag or office drawer.
3. Refuse plastic straws; use metal, bamboo, or silicone alternatives.
4. Bring your own container to restaurants for leftovers.

IMPACT STATISTICS:
- Replacing a single-use plastic bag habit with reusable bags saves ~700 bags per year per person.
- A reusable bottle eliminates ~156 plastic bottles per year.
- Refusing a straw saves ~1.8g of plastic per straw – small but multiplied by millions of users becomes significant.

GOVERNMENT SCHEMES (UK):
- The UK Plastic Packaging Tax (April 2022) charges manufacturers £210/tonne on plastic packaging with less than 30% recycled content.
- The Deposit Return Scheme (DRS) is being rolled out across England, Scotland, and Wales – consumers pay a small deposit on drinks bottles redeemed upon return.
- Scotland's single-use plastics ban (2022) prohibits plates, cutlery, balloon sticks, and polystyrene cups.

GOVERNMENT SCHEMES (US):
- Several US states (California, New York, Maine, Oregon) have enacted Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) laws requiring plastic producers to fund recycling infrastructure.
- California's SB 54 mandates that all plastic packaging sold in California must be 100% recyclable or compostable by 2032.
