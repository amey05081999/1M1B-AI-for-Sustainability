TOPIC: Eco-Friendly Product Recommendations and Green Shopping
SOURCE: Sustainable Consumer Guide

== ECO LABELS AND CERTIFICATIONS ==
Understanding eco-labels helps make informed purchasing decisions.

KEY ECO CERTIFICATIONS:
- FSC (Forest Stewardship Council): Timber, paper, and cardboard from sustainably managed forests.
- Rainforest Alliance Certified (green frog logo): Farms meeting environmental and social standards.
- Fair Trade: Fair wages, safe conditions, and community investment for producers.
- EU Ecolabel (flower logo): Products with reduced environmental impact across their lifecycle.
- GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard): Organic textiles produced to strict environmental standards.
- B Corp Certification: Companies meeting high standards of social and environmental performance.
- Leaping Bunny / Cruelty Free: Cosmetics and personal care not tested on animals.
- Energy Star (US): Energy-efficient electronics and appliances.
- EU Energy Label (A+++ to G): Mandatory rating on appliances sold in Europe.
- Marine Stewardship Council (MSC): Sustainably caught wild seafood.
- Aquaculture Stewardship Council (ASC): Responsibly farmed seafood.

== HOUSEHOLD CLEANING ==
Conventional cleaning products often contain harsh chemicals harmful to aquatic environments and packaged in single-use plastic.

ECO CLEANING PRODUCT RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Method, Ecover, Bio-D, Splosh: concentrated, biodegradable formulas often in recycled/recyclable packaging.
- Splosh refill pouches: concentrated sachets you add to water in a reusable bottle – up to 90% less plastic.
- DIY solutions: White vinegar (glass, surfaces), baking soda (scouring, deodorising), lemon juice (limescale), castile soap (general purpose).
- Cleaning tablets (e.g., Ocean Saver, Nimble): dissolve in water – eliminate plastic bottles entirely.
- Compostable cleaning cloths (e.g., Skoy cloths) replace 15 rolls of paper towels each.

== PERSONAL CARE ==
- Shampoo bars: Lush, Faith in Nature, Ethique. Last 60–80 washes; no plastic bottle.
- Bamboo toothbrushes: Humble Brush, Bambooth, Hydrophil. Biodegradable handle; nylon or castor-oil-based bristles.
- Safety razors: Edwin Jagger, Merkur, Leaf Razor. Stainless steel, lasts decades; only blade (recyclable) needs replacing.
- Reusable makeup remover pads: replace 1,000+ single-use cotton pads; wash in a mesh bag.
- Menstrual cups, reusable period pads, or period underwear: eliminate monthly disposable waste; menstrual cup lasts 10 years.
- Natural deodorant bars: Ben & Anna, Wild, Salt of the Earth – no plastic, no aluminium chlorohydrate.
- Solid sunscreen: new options from Shade and Sun Bum avoid chemical filters harmful to coral reefs.

== FASHION & CLOTHING ==
The fashion industry produces ~10% of global carbon emissions and uses ~93 billion cubic metres of water per year.

ECO FASHION TIPS:
1. Buy second-hand: Vinted, Depop, ThredUp, charity shops, car boot sales.
2. Choose quality over quantity; a durable item worn 30x has lower impact than fast fashion worn 5x.
3. Choose natural fibres: organic cotton, linen, hemp, Tencel (lyocell) over synthetic polyester/nylon (shed microplastics when washed).
4. Wash synthetics in a Guppyfriend bag to capture microplastic fibres.
5. Repair clothing: learn basic sewing (button, hem, patch) or use a local tailor.
6. Choose brands with strong sustainability credentials: Patagonia, Eileen Fisher, People Tree, Thought Clothing, Rapanui.
7. Organise or attend clothing swaps with friends, family, or community groups.
8. Donate to charity shops instead of binning unwanted clothes.

== HOME & GARDEN ==
- Eco paints: Earthborn, Little Greene, Auro – low or zero VOC (volatile organic compounds), natural pigments.
- Recycled content furniture: some IKEA lines, Biome, Neptune incorporate recycled or FSC-certified materials.
- Peat-free compost: all major brands now available peat-free (Westland, Dalefoot) – vital to protect peatland habitat.
- Rainwater butts: collect rain for garden watering, saving 100–200 litres on a single watering session.
- Drought-tolerant planting: lavender, sedums, grasses – reduces need for watering.

== ELECTRONICS ==
- Buy refurbished: Renewed electronics (Amazon Renewed, Back Market, Apple Certified Refurbished) use 60–80% less carbon to produce than new.
- Fairphone: ethically sourced, modular smartphone designed to be repaired and upgraded.
- Energy Star-rated products use 10–50% less energy than standard counterparts.
- Extend device life with cases, screen protectors, and battery replacements rather than upgrading.
